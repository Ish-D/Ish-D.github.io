#### Paper Controls:
Hover around the **corners** to edit 
- Outside a corner to **rotate**  
- On a corner to **scale**  
- Inside a corner to **drag**  

Hover around the **top middle** to
- Pin
- Delete

#### Canvas Controls
- Left click and drag to **pan**  
- Right click and drag to **rotate**  
- Scroll to **zoom**
