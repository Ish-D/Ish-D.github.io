# Umbrella

[[summary]]{A test page for a figure that lives in the text. An old man stands in the rain under an umbrella with his pipe, set in the seam between two columns, and he looks at your mouse wherever it is.}

---

[[viz(type: umbrella, place: gutter, width: 265px, height: 358px, pad: 24, dy: 8.75em, wrap: box)]]
[[drop(T)]]his page sets its text in two columns, and the drawing between them is a figure like any other on the site. It is placed with a line of text, the words curve around it, and the editor can move it.

Move the mouse around. He looks at it wherever it is, over the text, out in the margins or off the edge of the window, and he blinks now and then. Leave it alone and he goes back to glancing at the rain.

Rain that lands on the umbrella runs to the rim and drips off. Every so often a gust blows some of it in underneath, and it lands on his coat. Where a drop reaches the ground it leaves a ripple.

The smoke from his pipe rises until it meets the umbrella, spreads along the underside and drifts out from under it, and the wind pushes it about.

Everything in the drawing is the colour of the text, so it follows light and dark mode with the page. It keeps running while you scroll, and it stops when it is off screen or when your system asks for less motion.
}
