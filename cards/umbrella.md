# Umbrella

[[summary]]{A test page for a figure that lives in the text. A man stands in the rain under an umbrella with his pipe, set in the seam between two columns, and he looks at your mouse wherever it is.}

---

[[drop(T)]]his page sets its text in two columns, and the drawing between them is a figure like any other on the site. It is placed with a line of text, the words curve around it, and the editor can move it.

Move the mouse around. He looks at it wherever it is, over the text, out in the margins or off the edge of the window, and he blinks now and then. Leave it alone and he goes back to glancing at the rain.

Rain that lands on the umbrella runs to the rim and drips off. It all falls at one slant, and where a drop reaches the ground it leaves a ripple. In the puddle at his feet he shows upside down, umbrella and all, broken up by the water.

The smoke from his pipe rises until it meets the umbrella, runs up along the underside and drifts out from under it.

The drawing is the colour of the text, so it follows light and dark mode with the page. The one exception is his reflection, in ochre. The drawing keeps running while you scroll, and it stops when it is off screen or when your system asks for less motion.
}
